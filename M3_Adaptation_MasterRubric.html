THE ADAPTATION STUDIO · MASTER RUBRIC
What the Finished Work Is Graded On
Read this page at the start of Quarter 3, before you build. The sixteen milestones are each graded on completion: did you do the work, did you do it honestly, did you log your sources and your AI use. The finished work itself, the published kit you deliver in Quarter 4, is graded once, on the four bands below. Knowing them now tells you where to put your effort during production.
📦 WHAT YOU DELIVER IN QUARTER 4 (THE PUBLISHED KIT)
1. The work
Your film or documentary, as a narrated slide deck or video walkthrough of arranged reference images, captioned throughout.
2. Director's statement
Your interpretation of the unit's contested question and its defense from scholarship.
3. Annotated bibliography
JStor and primary sources, each annotated for what it gave the work.
4. Accuracy Statement
Sources named, dramatic license disclosed; the Source and Line Log attached.
5. AI-use log
Every milestone's line, in one place.
6. Live delivery
Your narration or a key monologue, spoken at the premiere. This is your Quarter 4 recitation.
📊 THE FOUR BANDS (25 POINTS EACH)
BandStrong (21–25)Developing (13–20)
Craft
film grammar (framing, sequence, shot choice); sound; pacing; captions		 Scene design, framing, sound, and pacing are deliberate and named in the statement using the film vocabulary correctly; the director's statement says what the medium did to at least one of those choices; captions are complete and readable; the work holds attention for its whole length. Choices are present but generic or unexplained; film vocabulary is misused or absent; sound is an afterthought; captions are partial; pacing sags or rushes.
Accuracy
Source and Line Log; Accuracy Statement; attribution		 Every textual reference (dramatic track) or factual claim (documentary track) traces to a logged source; both Accuracy Reviews are documented; the Accuracy Statement names sources and discloses every deliberate departure from them; on-screen attribution is present. The log has gaps; claims or lines appear that no source supports; departures from the sources are not disclosed; attribution is missing or partial.
Argument
the director's statement		 The statement poses the unit's contested question, takes a defensible position, shows how specific directorial choices render that position, and fairly states the strongest alternative reading before answering it. The statement summarizes the unit or describes the work without arguing an interpretation; choices are listed, not connected to a claim; no alternative reading is engaged.
Research
annotated JStor bibliography		 At least four credible sources, at least two from JStor, each annotated for what it contributed and how it was verified; the statement's interpretation is defended from this scholarship, not from summary; MLA is correct. Fewer or weaker sources; annotations describe the source but not its use; the statement leans on memory or summary rather than scholarship; citation errors.
Total: 100 pts

Below 13 in any band means the deliverable for that band is missing or does not function (no statement, no log, a work that will not play). The live delivery at the premiere is scored on the recitation thread's own 20-point rubric (accuracy, delivery, interpretation) and counts as the Quarter 4 recitation; it is not double-counted here.

📌 HOW THE MILESTONES AND THE RUBRIC FIT TOGETHER
Milestones (1–16): 15 points each, completion-weighted. Each asks: is the work present, honest, logged? A milestone is not graded on craft.
The kit (Quarter 4): 100 points, graded once on this page's four bands.
The premiere delivery: 20 points on the recitation rubric.
Put your production effort where the bands are: stage scenes you can explain (Craft), place nothing you cannot source (Accuracy), keep asking what your choices argue (Argument), and keep reading (Research).
🎬 THE FORMAT

Every production milestone this quarter uses one format: a narrated slide deck or a short video walkthrough of arranged reference images, captioned. The kit is graded on the four bands. The Craft band asks of the slides or video: is the viewer's attention placed on purpose, is sound used, is the pacing deliberate, are the captions complete.
