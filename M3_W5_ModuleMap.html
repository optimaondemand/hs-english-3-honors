WEEK 5 MAP
A Question Asked, and a Question Jane Asks Herself
Jane returns to Thornfield, and the orchard becomes the setting for a question that changes everything between her and Rochester. What does an answer given under strong feeling actually cost the person who gives it? What happens when a wedding does not go as planned? And when Jane's own comfort and her own conscience pull in opposite directions, which one does she trust? Read carefully this week: every chapter turns on a choice.
🎯 THE QUESTION BEHIND MODULE 3
What does it cost to stay true to one's conscience under the pressures of love, faith, and authority, and is it worth it?
📋 THIS WEEK
1
The Orchard Proposal (literature 1 of 3)
Jane names, out loud, what kind of self she insists on being. Read chapters 22 through 24: what does an offer made under strong feeling actually ask of the person receiving it?
2
The Wedding (literature 2 of 3)
Read chapters 25 and 26. What does the church scene reveal that changes what every earlier chapter meant?
3
"I Care for Myself" (literature 3 of 3)
Read chapter 27. Jane argues with herself before she argues with anyone else; what does she decide, and on what grounds?
4
Appositives and the Definition Move (grammar + vocabulary)
A sentence-level tool for defining something in the act of naming it, taught from this week's chapters; Word Study from chapters 22 through 27.
