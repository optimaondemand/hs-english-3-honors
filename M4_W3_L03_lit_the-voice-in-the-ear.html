📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Before Montag leaves, Faber presses something small and green into his hand: a way to keep talking, once the door closes.
Faber has already told Montag what a book holds. Before this visit ends, he goes one step further: he gives Montag a tiny two-way radio, worn in the ear, so that Faber can keep listening and coaching him after he leaves the house. Today's reading tool is telling the difference between guidance and a plan. A voice in your ear can steady you, correct you, even keep you company in a frightening moment, and still not amount to a decision about what to actually do. You read this scene for what it gives Montag, and, just as carefully, for what it does not yet give him.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to describe what the earpiece is, what it lets Faber do, and what Montag agrees to in accepting it.
2
I will be able to distinguish guidance from a plan and explain which one Montag actually has by the end of the visit.
3
I will be able to explain what dependence on the earpiece could cost Montag later, based on what you know of him so far.
📚 PART 1 · A WARM-UP BEFORE WE BEGIN
💭 QUICK CHECK-IN
Think of a time someone coached you through something difficult in real time, on the phone, over an earpiece, in your ear, while you were doing it. What did that help with, and what did you still have to do completely on your own?

Guidance versus a plan

Guidance is ongoing help: correction, encouragement, information delivered in the moment, from someone who can see or hear what you cannot. A plan is a decision about what will actually be done, by whom, and when, made in advance and capable of surviving the moment guidance goes silent. A person can have excellent guidance and still have no plan at all; the two are easy to mistake for each other because good guidance feels like progress. Read Montag's earpiece scene by asking, at the end of it, which of the two he has walked away with, and whether he, or the novel, seems to know the difference yet.

GUIDANCE OR A PLAN? A READING TOOL
Guidance
Ongoing help, in real time, from someone who can see or hear more than you can right now.
A plan
A decision, made in advance, about what will actually be done, that survives even if the guidance stops.
The trap
Good guidance can feel so much like progress that a person stops noticing no plan has actually been made.
By the end of today's reading, decide: does Montag leave Faber's house with a plan, or with a much better source of guidance and nothing more?
