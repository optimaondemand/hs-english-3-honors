What this milestone is
From rough cut to fine cut
●
Quarter 3 was production. Quarter 4 is post-production and release. This milestone carries out the revision plan you wrote in Milestone 12. You cut what the critique told you to cut, fix the pacing, add titles and on-screen citations, and finish the captions so a viewer with no sound, including your teacher, can follow every word. Keep the Master Rubric page open while you edit. Its Craft band asks whether pacing, sound, and captions are deliberate, and this is where they become so.
The medium. The work is a narrated slide deck, or a short video walkthrough of reference images arranged the way the scenes would be, with captions. The grade is for the pipeline and the thinking.

Proof the captions
Flag, verify, fix or keep, log
●
Use Claude, then log it. Paste your caption file, text only, into Claude and ask it to flag likely transcription errors, mistimed line breaks, and any place a quotation in the captions differs from the wording in your Source and Line Log.
