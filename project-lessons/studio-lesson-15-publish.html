See one statement opening
A model, and a check
WHAT A DIRECTOR'S STATEMENT OPENING LOOKS LIKE (A MODEL, NOT YOUR ANSWER)
Was Douglass's literacy a private triumph or a public argument? My adaptation takes the second position. I framed the schoolroom scene at eye level with the boys teaching him, not above them, because the historical record treats this moment as collective, not solitary. I wanted the image itself to argue that before the narration ever does. A skeptical viewer might reply that Douglass frames the moment as personal awakening. I answer that below, using his own later reflection.
QUICK CHECK · NOT GRADED

Proofread with Claude
Flags only, never rewrites
●
Use Claude, then log it. Give Claude your director's statement and ask for a final proofread pass looking for two things: any unsupported claim (a statement about the work's meaning or history with no source behind it) and any missing alternative reading (a reasonable objection to your interpretation that the statement never engages). Claude never writes or rewords the statement. It only points at what might be missing. Treat every flag as a question.
